Questions

Frequently asked

How much does it cost?

One flat plan at ₹899/month, billed annually. No setup fee, no maintenance charges, and 0% commission on your course fees — you keep every rupee students pay. Everything is included: your branding, your domain, unlimited courses, tests and students.

How long does it take to launch?

Usually within 24 hours of receiving your logo and colours. Adding your own courses takes only minutes each.

Do you take a cut of my fees?

No. You connect your own payment gateway and students pay you directly. We charge a flat ₹899/month (billed annually) — never a percentage of your income.

Can students watch on their phones?

Yes — any mobile browser, no app-store download. Students log in with just a phone number and OTP.

Can I upload PDFs and notes, not just video?

Absolutely — video lessons, PDF and Word notes, plus timed tests with ranks and percentiles.

Is my data and my students' data private?

Yes. Each institute's data is fully isolated, and you own it. You can export your students and payments any time.
