Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Plain-English summary: we only collect the contact details you choose to send us through the form, we use them to reach out about your demo and quote, and we never sell them.

Who we are

This page is operated by LaunchLMS ("we", "us"). If you have any questions about this policy, contact us using the WhatsApp or email details shared on the main page.

What we collect

When you fill in the "Launch My LMS" form, you choose to share:

  • Your name
  • Your WhatsApp number
  • Your email address
  • Your business type and approximate number of students

We may also collect standard, non-identifying analytics (such as pages viewed and general location) if analytics or advertising tags are enabled on this site, to understand how visitors find us.

How we use it

  • To contact you about a demo, walkthrough and a quote for your institute.
  • To answer your questions over WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • To improve our website and advertising.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it except with the trusted services we use to run this site and reach you (for example, our lead-email and messaging tools).

Cookies & advertising

If advertising tags (such as Google Ads or Google Analytics) are enabled, they may set cookies to measure the performance of our ads. You can control cookies through your browser settings at any time.

How long we keep it

We keep your enquiry details only as long as needed to follow up and, if you become a customer, to provide the service. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

Your choices

  • Ask us what information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Ask us to stop contacting you — just reply "STOP" on WhatsApp or email.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above will always reflect the current version.
